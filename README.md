## Key Findings
**Homelessness Trends (2014-2023)**

- U.S. Trend: Homeless population decreased slightly from 576,450 (2014) to 567,715 (2019) but increased to 653,104 (2023).
- Chicago Trend: Homeless population decreased by 16% (6,294 to 5,290 from 2014 to 2019) but rose again by 16% to 6,139 in 2023.

**Impact of Affordable Housing Shortage**

- Declining Affordable Housing Units:
  2014: 33 affordable homes per 100 extremely low-income renters
  2019: 31 per 100
  2023: 30 per 100
- Conclusion: The steady decline in affordable housing availability is a major factor contributing to homelessness.

**Wage Growth vs. Housing Affordability**

- Chicago's minimum wage rose from $8.25 (2014) to $13.00 (2023).
- Despite wage increases, rental costs outpaced income growth, worsening affordability.
- The end of the eviction ban (2023) further intensified rental demand and housing shortages, pushing more low-income renters into homelessness.
